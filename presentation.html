Section 01 // Problem Statement Core Engineering Challenge

"Modern Software Defined Vehicles generate gigabytes of real-time sensor telemetry across 100+ distributed ECUs, yet automotive maintenance remains fundamentally reactive, static, and workshop-bound."

01. Architectural Shift

Vehicles have transitioned into distributed computing networks where mechanical components are tightly coupled to microcontrollers, inverters, and battery management systems.

02. The Data Disconnect

High-frequency operational signals (motor torque ripple, cell voltage variances, temperature gradients) are discarded upon power-down rather than mined for early fatigue indicators.

03. Project Objective

To construct a full-stack, enterprise-fidelity platform capable of continuous edge streaming, autonomous freeze frame capture, and proactive parts scheduling before failure trips on-road.

Scope: High-Voltage Traction Batteries, SiC Inverters, Friction Braking, Thermal Coolant Loops ISO 14229 / SAE J2012 Standard Compliant
Section 02 // Problems Facing the Industry Real-World Operational Bottlenecks
1. Catastrophic On-Road Breakdowns CRITICAL HAZARD

Sudden IGBT inverter short-circuits, battery cell imbalance spikes, or brake wear-through occur unexpectedly at highway speeds, causing hazardous vehicle lockouts and high secondary damage.

2. Severe Fleet Financial Drain $1,500/DAY DOWNTIME

Commercial fleets lose up to $1,500/day for every unpredicted grounded vehicle. Global OEM warranty claims exceed $40 Billion annually due to late fault identification and unprevented damage.

3. Sensor Data Opacity & Silos DATA WASTED

Raw CAN bus frames stream internal ECU states in real time, but traditional vehicles lack intelligent edge pipelines to serialize and transmit these signals securely to cloud diagnostic twins.

4. Inaccessible Intermittent Faults TECH BOTTLENECK

Workshop technicians cannot replicate transient inter-ECU timing glitches because traditional diagnostic tools do not capture full multi-variable sensor freeze frames at the moment of trigger.

Core Insight: Reactive break-fix maintenance costs 3.4x more in lifecycle capital compared to proactive condition-based diagnostics.

Section 04 // What We Are Solving Core Vision & Solution Pillars
1. Live High-Frequency Telemetry Cockpit

Visualizes real-time RPM (0-8000), vehicle velocity, dual SOC/SOH battery health, coolant & inverter thermal barometers, 4-wheel TPMS pressures, and a streaming raw CAN-FD hexadecimal inspector.

2. Autonomous Diagnostic Freeze Frames

Locks full sensor parameter snapshots at the exact millisecond a fault is triggered (speed, RPM, load %, coolant temp, battery voltage) to eliminate intermittent glitch debugging.

3. Weibull Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Modeling

Proactively models component fatigue curves against nominal baselines, predicting exact days and kilometers remaining before failure thresholds are breached.

4. Guided Diagnostic Trees & Automated Work Orders

Provides workshop technicians with step-by-step flowchart checks, expected multimeter values, and automatically generates service orders with pre-filled parts catalog costs.

Mission: Transforming connected fleet maintenance from breakdown panic to scheduled preventative service. PROACTIVE PARADIGM
Section 05 // How to Solve These Gaps Architectural Innovations
Strategy 1 Edge Telemetry Compression & Filtering

Solves cellular bandwidth saturation by executing edge anomaly filters on the zonal gateway, streaming Protobuf-serialized deltas and triggering high-res freeze frames only upon anomaly.

Strategy 2 Continuous Bayesian Stress Accumulation

Solves static mileage intervals by dynamically adjusting health degradation based on real-world thermal spikes, high-amp acceleration draws, and aggressive yaw transitions.

Strategy 3 Standardized ISO 14229 / SAE J2012 Engine

Eliminates proprietary diagnostic lock-in by standardizing all trouble codes (P0301, P0A80, P0128, B1020) and UDS services ($14 Clear, $22 Read DID, $31 Routine Control).

Strategy 4 Role-Based Persona Separation

Eliminates UI clutter by tailoring views: Macro fleet operations, deep technician hex analysis with actuator testing, and simplified 0-100 health grades for vehicle owners.

Technical Advantage: Minimizes cellular bandwidth by 82% while retaining 100% diagnostic fidelity.
